Guía visual para Figma

Todos los componentes del sistema renderizados de verdad, con el CSS real del prototipo — los botones animan al pasar el cursor, el calendario muestra sus ocho estados, el chat está armado completo. Se abre en una ventana y Figma en la otra: cuando construyas una pieza, la ves funcionar aquí al lado mientras armas las variantes.

Cada bloque indica abajo su archivo suelto correspondiente en figma-parts/, por si prefieres importarlo con el plugin HTML to Design en lugar de reconstruirlo a mano.

Is the rate per night for the whole villa, or per person?

Per night, for the entire house — every suite included, however many of you travel, up to the villa's capacity. Rates are in US dollars and shown before the 21% Mexican tax.

What does the 21% tax add to the price I see?

Mexican lodging tax is added to every rate. Casa Cielo at $1,665 comes to $2,015 per night; Casa Coco at $4,840 comes to $5,856. Each villa card shows the total so there is no surprise at the end.

Is there a minimum stay?

Five nights at Thanksgiving and Spring Break, seven at Christmas and New Year. Outside those windows the minimum is shorter — the calendar shows it for the dates you pick.

My group is larger than one villa. What then?

Two pairs of houses share a wall and are sold as one compound with one contract: Lola & Encantada (13 suites, up to 26 guests) and Coco & Cielo (14 suites, up to 28). A combined stay works only when both houses are free on the same nights, which the calendar checks for you.

What is already included, and what costs extra?

Included: daily housekeeping, breakfast prepared by the in-house chef, paddle boards and kayaks, pre-stock service and airport transfers. On request: private chef dinners, yoga and wellness programmes, boat charters, excursions and event planning.

How do I know if my dates are free?

Availability is on this page. Pick your dates and group size and the calendar shows which houses are open — no email needed to find out.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

No payment and no card at this stage. A person replies within 24 hours with a formal quote for your dates. Holding a date requires a deposit, with the balance due 45 days before arrival.
